Rulecontainer Animals

  Rule	Carnivore
  If		Animal.Eats.Meat
  and		not Animal.Eats.Vegetables
  and		Animal.Has.PointyFangs
  then	Animal.Is.Carnivore

  Rule 	Omnivore
  If		Animal.Eats.Vegetable
  and		Animal.Eats.Meat
  then	Animal.Is.Omnivore

  Rule 	Herbivore
  If		not Animal.Eats.Meat
  and		Animal.Eats.Vegetable
  and		not Animal.Has.PointyFangs
  then	Animal.Is.Herbivore
